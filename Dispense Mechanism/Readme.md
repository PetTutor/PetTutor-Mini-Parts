## Build Instructions:  
- All 3D stl files are printed in PLA at .2 Layer Height and 15% infill
- BTum 4 is printed out of TPU at .2 layer height and 15% infill
- Silicon brushes are not printed and need to be purchased from PetTutor or you can download and print BTum - 5 out of TPU.  The TPU version do not last as long as the silicon brushes and will need to be changed often.
